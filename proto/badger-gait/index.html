Footfall pattern

Solid = stance (planted), hollow = swing (airborne). The gap between them is the duty factor.
What to look for: drop time scale to ~0.2× and watch a single leg. The paw should sit dead still through the solid bar, then flick forward through the hollow one — not swing evenly like a pendulum. Foot slip should stay under a few percent at any speed; if it climbs, stride rate has drifted out of step with speed. Try Silhouette only on the Game camera to check the gait still reads once the face stripes are gone.
